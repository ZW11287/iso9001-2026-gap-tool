The Three Biggest Gaps Organizations Are Missing

Based on our analysis of the transition timeline and UKAS Technical Bulletins:

1. Climate Change Risk (Clause 6.1)

Brand-new requirement. Your organizational context analysis must now include climate change as a factor — not optional, it's in the clause text itself. Most QMS auditors expect evidence you've addressed this.

2. Anti-Corruption Controls (Clause 5.1_new)

First-ever in any ISO management standard. Anti-bribery, fraud, and integrity requirements sit inside QMS scope. No equivalent existed in 2015 — so no existing documentation covers it. You need to build this from scratch.

3. Quality Culture as Measurable

Changed from implied to explicit. "Quality culture" is now a named, measurable requirement (Clauses 6.3 + 7.3) — leadership must show evidence through metrics, not just commitment statements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the gap-tool.

What is ISO 9001:2026 and why should I care?
ISO 9001:2026 is the first revision of the quality management standard since 2015. It introduces new requirements (climate change risk, anti-corruption controls) and reworks existing clauses. Organizations have until September 30, 2030 to transition from the 2015 version. If your clients or auditors already use or expect the 2026 standard, you need to start assessing your gaps now.
What Excel version do I need?
The workbook is compatible with Excel 365 (Microsoft 365), Excel 2019+, LibreOffice Calc, and Google Sheets. No macros or VBA are used — it relies entirely on standard spreadsheet formulas, which means no security warnings when opening the file.
Do I need to sign up or create an account?
No. The free Starter edition downloads directly from GitHub with no signup, no email collection, and no tracking. The tool works entirely offline once downloaded.
Can I use this for multiple organizations?
Yes — the free Starter edition covers a subset of clauses (4, 5.1–5.2) with no per-organization limits. For full access to all 59 clauses and the Action Plan template, see the Core tier (free at launch).
Does this replace a certification body's assessment?
No. This tool helps you conduct self-assessments and identify gaps before your certifier visits. It is not a substitute for professional certification-body assessment, nor does it guarantee compliance with any specific standard.
